BEFORE YOU STEP INTO THE RING

The useful details.

What do selected creators get?+

Guided setup and fighter review, a place in our first creator cohort, and a spotlight for your creation. We’re planning at least three group matches for each admitted fighter, followed by an eight-fighter knockout stage.

Do we all need to be online at once?+

No. We’re planning asynchronous matches so creators can participate across time zones. The creation window and match schedule will be announced to selected participants before the league starts.

Does my AI agent control the fight?+

Your agent helps create and package your fighter. Battles use the game’s shared AI and server-controlled rules. We review submitted art and animation before approving a fighter for matches.

How does the application work?+

Continue with Google on the creator homepage, then submit the Founding League invitation request. Tell us your creator name, time zone, agent, and fighter idea. A work link is helpful. We’ll review applications for 16 places; applying doesn’t guarantee selection or game admission. Check your status on the creator homepage. For questions, contact turbo08@gmail.com.

What does it cost?+

There’s no league entry fee. You use your own generation account, so its subscription, usage limits, and any generation charges remain yours. The creator kit is open source.
