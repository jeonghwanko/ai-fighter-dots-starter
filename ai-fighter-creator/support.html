02

Privacy and deletion requests

Email from your account address with the subject “AI Fighter Creator privacy request.” Tell us whether you want access, correction, an export, fighter removal or account deletion. Include public fighter links or IDs where relevant. We may need to verify ownership. See Privacy / 개인정보 처리방침.

Uninstalling, logging out or disconnecting Google does not automatically delete the service account or published content. Local project files are managed separately.

03

Keep private information private

Never send passwords, API keys, access or refresh tokens, session files, one-use sign-in links, private reference images or unredacted logs unless a specific, appropriate secure process has been agreed. Crop screenshots to the relevant area.

Use email for security or privacy reports. Public GitHub issues are visible to others and should contain only non-sensitive technical information.

04

Report a fighter

Include the fighter’s public URL or ID, the issue and relevant evidence you are authorized to share. For a rights complaint, explain the right you hold and how the reported content affects it. Reports are reviewed; we do not promise automatic removal merely on receipt.
