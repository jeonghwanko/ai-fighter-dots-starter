Before you step in.

Can I try it before making a fighter?

Yes. Start with the existing roster. Create a fighter whenever an idea strikes.

Do I need to know how to draw or code?

You can describe your idea and refine actual previews with a local creation assistant. It handles the files and tools; you choose the look and feel. The Docs explain what to install.

Is there an entry fee?

There is currently no club entry fee. The subscriptions, limits, and any usage charges of your own image and animation tools are separate.

Where do I start?

Open the creation guide, send the starter prompt to your local assistant, and describe a fighter. Start with one piece of concept art.
