ODDBOUT CLOUD PLUGIN

Your fighter. Connected.

Connect ODDBOUT to Dots with Google. No local MCP installation or API key.

정환 고 · 2026-10-24

01

What you can do

Read fighter requirements, check your account’s registration access, list your fighters, upload a reviewed package and follow its registration status.

02

Make it yours

Image generation, animation authoring, visual review and packaging use your chosen host and generation account. Connecting ODDBOUT does not provide a generation subscription or approve unfinished artwork.

03

Connect from Dots

Open ODDBOUT in Dots and choose Connect, sign in with the Google account that will own your fighters, then review and approve access. The public plugin is awaiting submission/review; it is not yet listed for everyone.

04

Register when ready

Ask to register the final package after reviewing it. The service validates all required files. Queued processing is not completed registration; check the final status. Registered fighter profiles and game assets are public.
