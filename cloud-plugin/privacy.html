ODDBOUT CLOUD PLUGIN

Privacy notice

How the ODDBOUT cloud plugin handles your account and character files.

정환 고 · 2026-10-24

01

Who provides this plugin

The public plugin is published by 정환 고 for ODDBOUT. The ODDBOUT service is operated by Lucas Foundation. Contact turbo08@gmail.com for privacy questions or data requests.

02

Information received

Google sign-in provides your account identity, basic profile and email. Provisioned email accounts can also sign in; their passwords are sent directly to Supabase Auth over HTTPS and are not included in connector requests. The connector receives OAuth authorization and the tool arguments and files selected for an operation: fighter metadata, manifests, animation assets, submission identifiers and status requests. It does not require your Google password, generation-provider credentials or a local PC installation.

03

Why we use it

We use this information to identify the owner, enforce access to their own submissions, validate packages, process registration, serve registered fighters and investigate service failures or abuse. The connector’s authorization cannot be used as a general database or storage login.

04

Providers and public data

Google provides social sign-in. Supabase hosts authentication, registration data and storage. Render forwards the public MCP requests, including OAuth access tokens, to the fixed Supabase MCP server and processes package validation. GitHub Pages hosts the connection and information pages. OpenAI operates Dots and the connector host. These providers may process data outside your country. Registered character profiles, artwork, animation and gameplay packages are public; sign-in email and authentication tokens are not part of a public fighter package.

05

Retention and choices

You can request fighter deletion in Dots. After ownership and deletion confirmation, it leaves your list, public rosters and new matches, and its account slot is released. Private original packages and historical records are retained; this is not file erasure. Previously issued asset links expire within 10 minutes. Contact support for account-data access, correction or erasure requests. Disconnecting alone does not delete fighters. Downloaded public copies cannot necessarily be recalled.

06

Contact safely

Include only the affected account email, public fighter ID and a short description. Never send passwords, OAuth callbacks, access tokens or signed download links. Changes to this notice will be published here.
