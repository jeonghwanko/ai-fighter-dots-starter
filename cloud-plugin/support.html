Contact

Email turbo08@gmail.com. Public plugin publisher: 정환 고. Include your language, the visible error, and the submission or fighter ID when available. Do not send passwords, tokens or OAuth callback URLs.

02

Connection expired

Return to the ODDBOUT plugin in Dots and start Connect again. Sign in with the account that owns your fighters. Use the EN / KR switch to change language without restarting an active request.

03

Registration is queued

Ask Dots to check your submission status. Validation can take time, especially while the free validation host starts. Reuse the same submission and retry key; do not regenerate your artwork because delivery or validation is waiting.

04

Package rejected

Review the validation reason and correct the affected package files. Revalidate before registering. A connection or valid JSON does not replace visual review of the animation.

05

Privacy and removal

Send account-data or fighter-removal requests to the same support address. Include only information needed to locate the record. We may need to verify ownership before changing data.
