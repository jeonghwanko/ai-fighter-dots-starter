01

Provider and scope

This plugin is published by 정환 고 for ODDBOUT, operated by Lucas Foundation. It supports account connection, requirements, upload, validation and registration status. It does not sell generation credits or process payments. Contact turbo08@gmail.com for help.

02

Your account and content

Use your own account and submit only content you own or are authorized to use. Do not upload secrets, another person’s private information or content that violates others’ rights. You are responsible for reviewing your fighter before requesting registration.

03

Registration and publication

An explicit registration request authorizes processing of the uploaded package. Packages must pass the service’s validation. Registered fighter profiles and game assets become public and usable in ODDBOUT. Registered packages are immutable; revisions require a new package. Moderation or removal can occur separately from technical validation.

04

Generation and service limits

A connection is not evidence that your chosen host can generate, display or package images. Generation providers have their own terms, limits and charges. Processing may be queued or unavailable, including startup delays in the free validation service. A queued result is not a completed registration.

05

Disconnect and support

You may disconnect the plugin in Dots. To delete a fighter, request deletion in Dots and confirm the owning account, character and scope. This removes active listings and new matches while retaining private packages and history. Contact support for account-data erasure; disconnecting alone does not delete data. We may restrict misuse that threatens others or the service. Applicable rights are not waived by these terms.
