01 / OUR VISION

Create new IP.
Explore its possibilities together.

Our goal is an agent community that creates IP and helps it reach people. You bring the idea and creative direction. Your agent helps you make and refine it, whether you are creating your first character or developing an existing world.

A first result is a beginning. Keep the parts you like, revise the movement and personality, then use the assets in a game, video or web project.

Every new project gives a character another chance to be discovered. The tools and experiments available today are our starting points toward that community.

02 / ONE SENTENCE. ONE IMAGE.

Turn an idea into a character that moves.

Start with a sentence or a character image. Work with your agent to turn it into artwork and AI animation.

  1. 01

    Share the idea

    “A cat in an oversized blue hoodie, travelling through an unfamiliar city.” Describe the appearance and personality, or provide your own character image. Review the artwork with your agent until it reflects the character you have in mind.

  2. 02

    Build the movement

    Create a stance, walks, runs and reactions for the scenes you want to express. Watch the actual previews to check that the face and clothing stay consistent and that the movement expresses your character’s personality.

  3. 03

    Continue into your next creation

    Keep the images and animations you choose as assets for your own games, videos and content. Develop the character’s story, create new scenes and explore ways to introduce its IP to people.

Connect through Grok, Codex, Claude or Gemini CLI to discuss your character and review the results. For local creation, oddbout-starter guides agents that can run local tools through artwork, animation and registration.

02

Create with your own generation accounts

Supported generation routes use ChatGPT/Codex or SuperGrok accounts. Connecting Claude or Gemini CLI does not itself provide image or video generation. Check oddbout-starter for the accounts and setup your route requires.

03

Keep what works. Refine what needs work.

Ask to keep the face and outfit while revising a walk or attack. Compare the previews and give feedback until the movement fits. Generation remains subject to your subscription and provider limits.

“Keep the face and blue hoodie. Make the walk more confident, and let the character return naturally to its starting stance after the attack.”

06 / BEFORE YOU CREATE

A few things to know.

Can I start with a character I already have?

Start with a character you created or an image you have permission to use. Describe its appearance and personality, then check that those qualities carry through to the animation. Image input depends on the creation route; the guide explains the connection and setup.

Does an AI subscription mean unlimited creation?

Repeated creation uses the allowances of your chosen subscription and generation provider. The connected agent and the account that generates images or video depend on the workflow. My fighters shows the current conditions for hosted creation; oddbout-starter explains local creation with your own accounts.

Can I use the assets in another project?

Download the generated assets or use the local output files in your own projects. Create from source material you have permission to use and follow the terms of your chosen generation service. Files entered into ODDBOUT tournaments must also meet the game’s asset specification.

Can I revise a registered character’s animation?

Use the local tools to revise the motions you need and update the character with a validated package. When an animation package changes, review the updated motions and enter again. This lets you improve its look and movement within the game’s shared rules.
